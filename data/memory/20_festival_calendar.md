## Standing rules
- Banks: closed every Sunday and every 2nd and 4th Saturday [verified: RBI]. UPI / net-banking fee payments still
  work on holidays; cheque / DD / RTGS clearing slips to the next working day.
- Do not set application or fee deadlines inside Navratri (11-20 Oct 2026) or Diwali week (6-14 Nov 2026); land them
  3-5 working days before Navratri, or after Labh Pancham (14 Nov 2026) when Gujarat businesses reopen.
- Gujarat school / college Diwali vacation typically runs ~2 weeks around Diwali (late Oct to mid-Nov) [verify per institution].
