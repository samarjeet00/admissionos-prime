## Standing rules
- Do not set application or fee deadlines inside Navratri or Diwali week; land them 3-5 working days after Labh Pancham or after Navratri ends, when families re-engage.
- Gujarat banks and offices are also closed on 2nd / 4th Saturdays; UPI and online fee payment still work, but cheque / DD payments slip to the next working day.
