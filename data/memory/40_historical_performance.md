# Historical performance benchmarks (derived 15 Sep 2026 from the "Date Wise Performance Comparision - CCC" workbook)
Source: one-off analysis of the Domestic REG / ADM, Goa, Online and Last Dates tabs. Figures are session totals
summed from the day-wise grids; a session runs Sep (prev year) to Feb (intake year + 1). "Through Aug" = like-for-like
comparison point. Refresh these numbers from the sheet or the portal when asked - they are a snapshot.

  then Apr -14%, May -3%, Jun -14% registrations; Jun admissions -11%. Jul/Aug slightly up.

## CCC yield (downstream confirmation stage tracked in the CCC tab; share of admissions reaching CCC)
- 2025-26: 66% of admissions for the season; 67% for Sep-Apr. 2026-27 Sep-Apr: 64% (volume +14%, yield -3 pts).
- Peak-month yield fell in 2026-27: Apr 62% (vs 68%), May 58% (63%), Jun 56% (67%), Jul 55% (67%). CCC lags admissions, so the latest months understate.

## Deadline-day effect (registrations on the last date)
- Deadline days produce 200-700 registrations (3-10% of the month in one day). 2026-27: 31 Mar 213, 21 Apr 247, 23 May 509, 27 May (ext) 291.
- A single 5-day extension yields 35-75% of the original deadline day (best: 2024-25 May 10th 403 + 15th 439). Second extensions were never used - keep it that way.

## Goa campus (2 seasons)
- 2025-26: ~1,130 registrations / ~770 admissions. 2026-27 (to date): 2,465 / 1,755 -> +118% / +127%, conversion 71%.
- Goa peaks Mar-Apr (REG 507 / 612; ADM 353 / 448), two months earlier than Vadodara (May-Jun).

## Online programmes (registrations)
- July intake: 2025-26 4,296 vs 2026-27 4,319 (flat; May -19%, Jun -7%, Jul +28%).
- January intake: 2024-25 1,007 vs 2025-26 2,011 (+100%); Feb-Mar are its peak months.
