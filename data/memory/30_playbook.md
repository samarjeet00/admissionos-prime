# Admissions playbook (edit as leadership decides)
- Standard follow-up cadence for a fresh lead: first contact within 1 hour, 3 attempts in 48 hours, WhatsApp + SMS nudge, call-back scheduled.
- Lead health definitions: Fresh = created within 7 days and contacted; Stale = no interaction for 14+ days; Dormant = 30+ days; Hot = document verification or fee stage started.
- Admission Readiness Score weights (default): Lead Health 25, Exam / Result Status 15, Festival Impact 10, Team Capacity 20, Conversion Probability 30.
- Deadline extension policy: extend at most once per programme per cycle; announce 5+ days before the current deadline.
